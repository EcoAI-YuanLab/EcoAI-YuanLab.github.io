Rijul Dimri is a postdoctoral researcher in the EcoClimate AI Lab under the supervision of Dr. Yuan. His research focuses on developing physics-informed AI models for predicting hydrological extreme events and understanding the underlying processes, with an emphasis on improving model interpretability and gaining scientific insights into coastal flooding events.

Rijul received his Ph.D. from the [Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Houston](https://www.uh.edu/nsm/earth-atmospheric/), under the supervision of [Prof. Yunsoo Choi](https://www.uh.edu/nsm/earth-atmospheric/people/faculty/yunsoo-choi/). His doctoral research focused on developing interpretable and scalable AI frameworks for understanding and forecasting complex environmental processes.
