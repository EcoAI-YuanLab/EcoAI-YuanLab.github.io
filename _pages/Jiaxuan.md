Jasmine (Jiaxuan) Miao is a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Houston](https://www.uh.edu/nsm/earth-atmospheric/). She earned her Master’s degree from [UCLA Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences](https://atmos.ucla.edu/), where she studied noctilucent cloud formation. Her research interests include wildfire dynamics and post-fire impacts, AI, and process-based environmental modeling. Outside the lab, she is a proud cat mom, an avid hiker and kayaker, and a huge baseball fan.
