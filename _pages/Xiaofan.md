Xiaofan Shen is a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Houston](https://www.uh.edu/nsm/earth-atmospheric/). She completed her master’s degree at the [Yale School of the Environment](https://environment.yale.edu/) and has a background in urban forestry and environmental sciences. Her research interests focus on understanding the interactions among ecosystems, climate, and human-modified landscapes across different scales using remote sensing, geospatial analysis, and machine learning approaches. Beyond her academic work, Xiaofan enjoys traveling, exploring natural landscapes, swimming, cooking, and music.
